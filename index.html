    Model Audit &
    Limitations

    Zero-Cost & On-Device Architecture: Origin Check runs completely inside the visitor's browser using WebAssembly via Transformers.js. Public ONNX model weights are retrieved once from public CDN repositories and cached locally in your browser's Cache API / IndexedDB. No user text or files ever leave your device.

    Text Detector Audit: Powered by onnx-community/chatgpt-detector-roberta-ONNX (derived from Hello-SimpleAI/chatgpt-detector-roberta).
    License: CC-BY-SA 4.0 (inherited from HC3 training data).
    Known Limitations: Trained exclusively on early 2023 ChatGPT question-answering data. On our independent held-out evaluation across modern LLMs (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Llama 3, Mistral Large), it achieved only 42.9% raw accuracy (47.9% balanced accuracy) with an 87.5% false-negative rate on modern generators and a 16.7% false-positive rate on authentic human writing. Paraphrased AI text completely evaded detection.

    Image Screening Audit: Uses onnx-community/Deep-Fake-Detector-v2-Model-ONNX (ViT-base, Apache 2.0).
    Domain Scope: This model is trained on facial deepfakes and face swaps. It is NOT a general AI-image detector for landscapes, diffusion art, or illustrations. Pixel screening on non-face images is experimental and outside tested scope; for general media, the tool surfaces embedded metadata (PNG generation parameters, camera EXIF, and C2PA credentials).

    Guardrails & Inconclusive Outputs: Inputs under 35 words lack statistical entropy and are designated Inconclusive. Non-facial images outside tested model scope are flagged as experimental. Output scores are statistical signals, not probabilities, and must never be used as singular evidence in academic, employment, or legal decisions.
